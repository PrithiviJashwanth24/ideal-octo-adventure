## OUR SOLUTION

FitCheck is built on 7 core intelligence layers:

### 1. AI Wardrobe Digitization
Vision AI scans, categorizes, and creates rich metadata for every clothing item. Detects brand, fit, color, material, occasion-suitability, estimated value, sustainability score, and style archetype.

### 2. The FitCheck AI Stylist
Context-aware outfit recommendations powered by GPT-4o Vision. Considers:
- Calendar & events
- Weather
- Social repetition history
- Confidence patterns
- Body type & style DNA

Generates: Safe Fit, Statement Fit, Stealth Luxury, Date Night, Boardroom, Airport, Wedding fits — with AI rationale for each.

### 3. Style DNA Engine
Builds a style embedding (vector) for each user based on wardrobe data, wear patterns, confidence history, and stated preferences. Identifies archetype (Quiet Luxury, Old Money, Streetwear, etc.) and generates personalized style insights.

### 4. Social Memory Layer
Tracks what was worn, where, with whom, and on what social platforms. Prevents social repetition. Creates an Event-to-Outfit graph.

### 5. Closet Intelligence
Monthly reports showing: utilization rates, cost-per-wear, neglected items, brand distribution, diversity scores, sustainability ratings, and ROI analysis.

### 6. AI Shopping Engine
Detects wardrobe gaps. Recommends specific products. Integrates with Zara, Nike, Farfetch, Myntra, SSENSE, Amazon Fashion. Generates AI wishlists.

### 7. Donation + Resale Engine
Identifies items for donation, recycling, or resale. Connects to platforms. Gamifies sustainability.
