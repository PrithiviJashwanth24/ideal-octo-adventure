## PHASE 1: WAITLIST & CREATOR LAUNCH (Month 0-3)

### The Virality Mechanism
FitCheck's core viral loop: **Scan → Discover → Share**

1. User scans wardrobe (curiosity trigger)
2. AI generates "Wardrobe Score" + Style DNA
3. System generates shareable "Wardrobe Wrapped" card
4. User shares → friends download → cycle repeats

## RETENTION ARCHITECTURE

### Habit Loops
1. **Morning**: "Your AI Stylist picked today's fit" (push at 7am)
2. **Evening**: "Log today's outfit" (push at 8pm)
3. **Weekly**: "Your Style Report is ready" (Sunday morning)
4. **Monthly**: "Wardrobe Intelligence Report"
5. **Annual**: "Wardrobe Wrapped — Your year in style"

### Gamification
- **Outfit Streaks**: Log 7 days in a row → badge + points
- **Style Challenges**: "No Repeat Week", "Color Challenge", "Sustainable April"
- **Wardrobe Health Goals**: "Improve your Closet Health Score from 62 → 75"
- **Community Leaderboards**: Style Circles compete for best average confidence score

### Emotional Investment Triggers
- More items → richer AI = better recommendations (ownership effect)
- Social connections = social switching costs
- Outfit history = irreplaceable personal data
- Style DNA = identity investment ("that's who I am")
