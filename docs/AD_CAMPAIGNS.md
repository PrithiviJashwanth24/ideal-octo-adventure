# FITCHECK — AD CAMPAIGNS & CREATIVE DIRECTION
## Performance Marketing Playbook

---

## BRAND IDENTITY

**Tone:** Intelligent. Aspirational. Quietly confident.
**Not:** Try-hard, budget fashion, fast fashion energy.

**Visual Language:**
- Dark backgrounds (#0A0A0B) with gold accents (#D4AF37)
- Cinematic 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Reels/TikTok
- Typography: Clean, wide-set sans-serif (editorial feel)
- Imagery: Architecture, luxury objects, minimal fashion photography
- No stock photos. Real wardrobes. Real people.

## INFLUENCER BRIEF TEMPLATE

**For: @[Handle], FitCheck Partnership**

Thank you for partnering with FitCheck. Here's how to authentically showcase the product:

**The Story Arc:**
1. Setup the problem (wardrobe overwhelm, decision fatigue, social repetition)
2. Show the discovery (download, first scan, wow moment)
3. The reveal (Style DNA, wardrobe stats, outfit recommendations)
4. The transformation (how it changed your morning routine / confidence)

**Key Messages to Hit:**
- "FitCheck isn't a fashion app — it's an AI that understands your entire wardrobe"
- The Style DNA analysis (very shareable moment)
- Outfit recommendations that are smart + contextual
- Cost-per-wear revelation (relatable financial shock)

**DO:**
✓ Show your real wardrobe (authenticity builds trust)
✓ React genuinely to the AI's analysis
✓ Show the Style DNA card (very shareable)
✓ Use #FitCheckWrapped in December content

**DON'T:**
✗ Call it a "fashion app" (it's an AI system)
✗ Show only flattering results (honest > perfect)
✗ Force it — the product sells itself when shown authentically

**Deliverables:** 1 TikTok (60s+) + 3 Instagram Stories + 1 Instagram Reel

## YOUTUBE STRATEGY

**Channel Concept: "Dressed by AI"**

Series: FitCheck takes over someone's wardrobe for 30 days. Documents the transformation.

**Episode Structure (15-20 min format):**
- Day 1: Current state (wardrobe scan, the ugly stats)
- Day 7: First AI outfit successes (and failures)
- Day 15: Style DNA reveal (emotional moment)
- Day 30: Final transformation (before/after, confidence interview)

**Guest Pipeline:** Entrepreneurs, creatives, professionals who want to elevate style

**Target:** 100K subscribers, 3M views per episode at scale
